Research

With Prof. Jaromir Savelka at CMU's TEEL Lab.

Learning to Escalate: Do LLM Agents Generalize Governance Policy to Unseen Rules?

under review, ICLR 2027

We train Qwen2.5-7B tool agents with SFT and then GRPO, holding whole rule families out of training. Restraint transfers to rules the model never saw, but escalation collapses from 99% to 8%, a failure that violation-rate metrics miss.

Towards Spec Learning: Inference-Time Alignment from Preference Pairs

under review, NeurIPS 2026 · arXiv

We compile a short instruction and a handful of preference judgments into a natural-language "spec" that steers a model at inference time, with no weight updates. On specialized domains it often beats direct preference optimization trained on far more data, and the spec stays human-readable.

Semantic Span Annotation: An Exploratory Study of LLM Annotation

ACL 2026, Student Research Workshop · paper

A study of when handing a model a structured definition of a label helps it annotate expert text, and when it quietly hurts.

Now working on rubric-based reward design for GRPO in hard-to-verify domains, including generating rubrics from preference pairs.
